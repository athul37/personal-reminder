Using the app without an account

Everything you enter is saved in your browser's storage on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Clearing your browser's data deletes your payments. Safari can also clear a website's data if you don't open it for about a week; adding Payments Ahead to your Home Screen avoids this.
  • Use Settings › Export Payments to keep a backup.

What the app accesses if you sign in with Google

  • Your email address (openid, userinfo.email). Used to show which account you're signed in with and to keep accounts separate on a shared device.
  • A private app-data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). Your payment list is saved there as one file. The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar, only if you turn on reminders (calendar.app.created). The app creates one calendar called "Payments" and adds events to it. It cannot read or change your other calendars or events.

The app does not request your name, profile picture, contacts or any other Google data.

Where your data goes

  • Your browser. A copy of your payment list is kept on your device so the app opens quickly and works offline, along with the email of the last account used (to offer "Continue as…"). Both are removed when you sign out or delete your data.
  • Your Google account, if you sign in: Drive and, if you turn it on, Calendar, as described above.
  • Directly to Google, never through us. Data travels between your browser and Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Sign-in access is kept only in the app's memory, expires within about an hour, and is never saved.
  • Calendar links and files. If you use "Add to Google Calendar" or download the calendar file (.ics), you choose to send those payment details to your calendar. We never see them.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent or share your data. We don't use it for advertising, profiling or analytics, or to train AI or machine-learning models, and no person reads it.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data at any time in the app.
  • Take a copy: Settings › Export Payments (.json).
  • Delete everything: Settings › Delete All My Data. This removes the file from your Google Drive, removes the "Payments" calendar, clears this device and disconnects the app from your Google account.
  • Withdraw access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also delete the saved file in Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › Payments Ahead › Options › Delete hidden app data.
  • Complain: contact us first. You may also complain to your data-protection authority.

How long data is kept

We keep nothing. Your data stays on your device or in your Google account until you delete it. Google handles deletion from its own systems under the Google Privacy Policy.

Hosting

The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads the app, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This never includes your payments.

Security

The app has no server to break into. It asks Google for the narrowest permissions that work, uses encrypted connections only, and allows only its own code and Google's sign-in library to run. No system is perfectly secure, so protect your Google account with 2-Step Verification and sign out on shared devices.
